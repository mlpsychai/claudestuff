# House Style Overlays

The APA Publication Manual (7th ed.) is the primary authority for all formatting and citation. This file captures the user's personal stylistic preferences that **supplement** APA conventions, and in the rare case of conflict, **override** APA for the user's own manuscripts.

When a house rule overrides an APA convention, surface that override in the *Style notes* block of your response so the user can verify the deliberate departure.

---

## Punctuation

### Em dashes
Forbidden in body prose. The single permitted exception is one terminal em dash used to conclude a sentence for emphasis, sparingly (no more than once or twice per page). Within sentences, prefer commas, parentheses, or colons.

*Departure from APA:* APA permits em dashes liberally for parenthetical asides. This rule is tighter.
